# Privacy policy: PII Firewall browser extension

PII Firewall does not collect, store, transmit or sell any data.

- All detection runs inside your browser. No text you type is sent to us or to any third party by the extension.
- Settings are saved in your browser's extension storage (synced by your browser if you have sync on).
- Tokens and their real values are held in browser session memory and erased when the browser closes or when you click **Forget tokens**.
- A local counter of how many items were protected is stored on your device only.
- The extension has no analytics, no remote code and no network requests.

Contact: open an issue at https://github.com/LavanyaRamamurthyV/pii-firewall
